An iOS app that reminds you to water your plants on time, so you can take care of them without forgetting.

## Main Features
- Add plants with name, room, light, watering days, and water amount
- Edit or delete any plant (button or swipe to delete)
- Check off plants you've watered
- Animated progress bar showing how many plants were watered
- Smart screens: Start, plant list, All Done, and No reminders
- Local notifications based on each plant's watering schedule
- Dark mode with custom colors and glass-effect buttons

## Technologies Used
- Swift
- SwiftUI
- Xcode
- MVVM Architecture
- UserNotifications (local notifications)
